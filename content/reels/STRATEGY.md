# CJ Studio — Reels Content Strategy

**Purpose:** Reels are the agency's lead funnel. Instagram payouts are invite-only and unreliable — we do not chase them. Every reel exists to do one of two jobs:

1. **Drive the 48-Hour Website Sprint** — £1,995 fixed price, delivered in 48 hours (live on cjcreativestudio.com).
2. **Build the audience that buys future digital products** — templates, courses, agent tooling.

If a reel idea doesn't ladder to one of those two, we don't film it.

---

## Positioning

**The AI-speed web agency.** CJ Studio ships agency-grade websites in 48 hours because the pipeline is AI-native: Claude Code agents scaffold and iterate, humans direct design and taste, Vercel deploys on push. Nobody in the UK local-business market is showing this. That gap is the content.

One line, used everywhere: **"Agency-quality websites, built in 48 hours."**

| Audience | What they want | What they buy |
|---|---|---|
| **UK local businesses** (trades, restaurants, clinics, gyms — the LA Roofing profile) | Proof we're real, fast, and worth £1,995 | The Sprint |

The pillars below serve both without splitting the account: local businesses see *results*, the dev audience sees *how*. The overlap (speed) is the brand.

## The 3 Content Pillars

### Pillar 1 — Client-Site Speed Builds *(proof of the Sprint)*
Screen-recorded builds of real client sites, compressed into 20–40 second timelapses. LA Roofing, Range Shipping, PadelVision, and every future client. The message is always the same: *this went from nothing to live in 48 hours.*
**Primary CTA:** DM "SPRINT".

### Pillar 2 — Before/After Transformations *(pain → outcome)*
The template-site graveyard vs. what we ship. Old vs. new, slow vs. fast, generic vs. designed. Includes feature spotlights that function as before/afters (the LA Roofing cost calculator vs. a dead "Contact Us" form). This pillar speaks most directly to UK local businesses.
**Primary CTA:** DM "SPRINT" / link in bio.

### Pillar 3 — Behind the Scenes of the AI Agent Operation *(the moat)*
Ollie's actual agent infrastructure: the Jarvis dashboard dispatching tasks to Claude Code agents, worktrees building overnight, and the trading platform the same agents run. This is the build-in-public pillar — it earns follows, credibility, and the future-products audience. It also makes the 48-hour claim believable instead of salesy.
**Primary CTA:** Follow / comment keyword.

**Rotation:** never post the same pillar twice in a row. Default weekly shape: Speed Build → BTS → Before/After (→ flex slot in 4-post weeks).

## Cadence

**3–4 reels per week.** Suggested slots: Mon / Wed / Fri, plus an optional Sunday flex post (repost top performer, react to a result, or a raw talking-head).

Sustainable because of the capture rule: **OBS records every client build by default** (see TEMPLATE.md). Filming is a byproduct of doing the work — editing is the only added cost, budget ~45–60 min per reel.

---

## Format rules (every reel)

- **Hook lands inside 1.5 seconds** — spoken + on-screen text simultaneously. No logos, no intros, no "hey guys".
- **20–40 seconds** target length. Under 20 for pure timelapses is fine.
- **Readable on mute:** on-screen text carries the whole story; voiceover is a bonus layer.
- **9:16 vertical.** Screen recordings get cropped/zoomed to the action, not letterboxed — record at high res and punch in.
- **One CTA per reel.** Never stack "follow AND DM AND link in bio".
- **Captions:** first line restates the hook (it's the preview text), 2–4 short lines of substance, CTA last. Write for Instagram keyword search — say "web design", "website", "UK" in plain words.
- **Hashtags:** 4–6, mixed altitude — 2 niche (#webdesignuk #nextjs), 2 audience (#buildinpublic #uksmallbusiness), 1–2 local when relevant (#exeter #devon). Hashtags are a minor lever; the caption keywords and hook do the work.
- **Audio:** trending audio low under VO when it fits; never let it dictate the edit.

## Production constraints (hard rules)

- Everything filmable with **OBS screen recording + a phone**. No drone shots, no studio, no actors, no motion-graphics packages.
- Phone use: talking-head selfie clips, hands-on-keyboard b-roll, phone-in-hand scrolling the live site (shot with the phone propped or by a second phone/OBS webcam).
- **Client & data hygiene:** never show client email threads, invoices, or private dashboards. Trading platform shots show **paper trading / backtests only**, with a "not financial advice" caption line. Get client OK before featuring their build (checklist item in TEMPLATE.md).

## CTA system

| CTA | Use on | Funnel |
|---|---|---|
| **DM "SPRINT"** | Pillars 1 & 2 | Reply with the Sprint one-pager + booking link. This is the primary lead capture — track every SPRINT DM. |
| **Link in bio** | Offer-explainer reels | Bio link → cjcreativestudio.com (point at the Sprint section/page once its URL is final). |
| **Follow / comment keyword** | Pillar 3 | Audience compounding for future products. |

## Measurement (weekly, 15 minutes)

Track per reel: 3-second hold %, average watch %, follows per reel, **SPRINT DMs**, link taps. Decision rule: double down on the pillar that produced DMs, not the one that produced views. A 2,000-view reel that books one Sprint beats a 200k-view reel that doesn't.

## Tone

Direct, confident, a bit dry. Show, don't claim — the screen recording *is* the argument. No hustle-bro energy, no "🚀 game-changer" language. British understatement is the brand: "Built in 48 hours. Here's the recording."
