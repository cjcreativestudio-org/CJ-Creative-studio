## Rules while executing

- **Never two consecutive posts from the same pillar** — the Mon P1 / Wed P3 / Fri P2 shape guarantees this.
- **If a real build is happening that week, it jumps the queue.** A live client Sprint filmed with TEMPLATE.md beats any pre-planned script — push the scripted post back a slot.
- **Post times:** 12:00–13:00 or 18:00–19:30 UK. Consistency matters more than the exact hour.
- **After each post:** 15 minutes replying to every comment and DM. SPRINT DMs get the one-pager + booking link same day.
- **End of cycle:** run the Measurement review in STRATEGY.md, then rebuild this calendar for the next 30 days — heavier on whichever pillar produced DMs.
