# 06 — The Button That Books Roofs

**Pillar:** P2 Before/After (feature spotlight) · **Length:** ~25s · **Audience:** UK local businesses
**Assets:** la-roofing.vercel.app cost calculator, any generic "Contact Us" form for contrast

## Hook (first 1.5s)

**Spoken + on-screen:**
> "This one button gets a roofer more leads than his entire old website did."

Visual: OBS on the LA Roofing calculator, cursor pressing the CTA.

## Shot list

| # | Time | Shot |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0–4s | OBS: LA Roofing "Calculate Your Roof Cost" — click it, options appear. Punch in tight |
| 2 | 4–8s | OBS contrast: a bare "Contact us" form (template demo or a mocked plain form on localhost) — cursor hovers, doesn't fill it, leaves. Text: "what everyone else has" |
| 3 | 8–16s | OBS: back on the calculator — full flow: pick roof type → size → estimate appears. Real-time, no cuts, ~8s of genuine product use |

| 5 | 21–25s | OBS: scroll from the calculator into the reviews/gallery. End card |

## Voiceover

> "This one button gets a roofer more leads than his entire old website did.
> Most trades sites end with 'contact us'. Nobody fills that in — it feels like commitment.
> A calculator is different. It's a game. Roof type, rough size… and there's your ballpark price.
> No phone call. No waiting. And the roofer gets a lead who already knows the number.
> This is what your website is supposed to do: not sit there — capture."
