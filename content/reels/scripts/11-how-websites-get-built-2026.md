## Voiceover

> "This is how a website actually gets built in 2026. No agency will show you this.
> Step one: the brief. Everything starts with what the business actually needs to say.
> Step two: AI agents scaffold the whole build — structure, pages, components. What used to take three weeks takes an afternoon.
> Step three: the part AI can't do — taste. We push every detail until it feels right.
> Step four: push, and it's live worldwide in about a minute.
> That's the whole secret. Machines for speed, humans for judgement. That's why it's 48 hours and not 8 weeks."
